# FMCG Sales & Marketing Analytics | Power BI

## Project Overview

This project analyzes FMCG sales and marketing performance from 2023 to 2025 using Power BI. It focuses on revenue growth, profitability, promotion effectiveness, and marketing efficiency to support data-driven business decision-making.

The project follows an end-to-end analytics workflow, from source data preparation in Power Query to KPI development, dashboard reporting, and management recommendations.

## Business Objectives

1. Analyze sales performance and identify opportunities to increase revenue.
2. Evaluate promotion performance using sales and profitability metrics.
3. Assess sales channel performance and marketing spend efficiency.

## Business Questions

- How did net sales and profitability change from 2023 to 2025?
- Which products contribute most to total net sales?
- Which sales channels generate the strongest revenue and profit margins?
- How do promotion types differ in profitability?
- Which channels and product categories require closer profitability review?

## Tools & Skills

- **Power BI Desktop** — Data modeling, DAX measures, interactive dashboards
- **Power Query** — Data cleaning and transformation
- **DAX** — KPI calculations and time-based analysis
- **Business Analysis** — Findings, insights, and recommendations
- **Data Storytelling** — Management-focused reporting

## Key Findings

- Total Net Sales reached approximately **$14.45 million** over the analysis period.
- Total Net Profit was approximately **$3.31 million**, with a Net Profit Margin of **22.90%**.
- Annual net sales increased from approximately **$4.59 million in 2023** to **$5.02 million in 2025**, although the annual growth rate slowed.
- Wholesale and Distributor channels contributed approximately **66.86% of total net sales**.
- The Online channel had a lower Net Profit Margin than the other analyzed channels.
- Beverages generated substantial sales but had a comparatively low Net Profit Margin.
- Promotion types showed different profitability outcomes, highlighting the importance of evaluating both sales and profit.

## Recommendations

- Monitor monthly revenue growth by channel, region, and product.
- Review Online channel profitability, marketing spend, and discounting.
- Evaluate promotions using both net sales and net profit.
- Investigate pricing, discounts, and cost components in the Beverages category.
- Review negative-profit records to identify recurring business patterns.
